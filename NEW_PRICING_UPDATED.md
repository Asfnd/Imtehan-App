## 📊 Psychological Pricing Benefits

### **Why Rs. 999 / Rs. 1,499 / Rs. 1,999 is perfect:**

1. **Ends in 9** - Psychological pricing trick
   - Rs. 1,999 feels like "Rs. 1,000-range" not "Rs. 2,000+"
   - Brain rounds down unconsciously
   - 2-5% better conversion than round numbers

2. **Clear Value Ladder**
   - Rs. 999 → Rs. 1,499 → Rs. 1,999
   - Each tier has clear savings incentive
   - Annual plan saves Rs. 2,000 vs quarterly plan

3. **Under Rs. 2,000 barrier**
   - Rs. 1,999 stays under the Rs. 2,000 mental threshold
   - "Under Rs. 2,000" sounds affordable
   - "Over Rs. 2,000" triggers price resistance

4. **Monthly cost messaging**
   - Rs. 167/month = less than Rs. 200/month
   - "Less than a pizza per month"
   - Easy for students to justify
