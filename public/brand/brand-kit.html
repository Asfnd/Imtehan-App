Where to upload

When you upload a new profile picture, pick the downloaded .png (not the SVG). Instagram: Profile → Edit profile → change photo. WhatsApp, X, Facebook, LinkedIn: use PNG the same way. The book (light) or gradient orb stay clearest if the two-line wordmark feels too small in the little circle on mobile.
